# Local inference engine marks

- `llama-cpp.svg` is from [ggml-org/llama.brand](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.brand). The repository's [brand usage terms](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.brand/blob/master/BRAND-USAGE.md) explicitly allow redistribution in software when used to identify or refer to Llama.cpp. The asset remains unmodified.
- `vllm.svg` is the compact official mark from [vllm-project/media-kit](https://github.com/vllm-project/media-kit), which describes its contents as official vLLM logo assets. The asset remains unmodified.
- `exllama-v3.png` is the unmodified ExLlamaV3 logo published at `doc/logo.png` in [turboderp-org/exllamav3](https://github.com/turboderp-org/exllamav3/blob/master/doc/logo.png). The horizontal mark is displayed at its original aspect ratio in the UI.
